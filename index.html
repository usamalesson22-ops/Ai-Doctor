ℹ️ Description

Early blight is a fungal disease caused by Alternaria solani. It appears as dark brown spots with concentric rings on older leaves, eventually causing yellowing and defoliation.

1

Apply copper-based fungicide (Copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 2g/L water)

2

Remove affected lower leaves immediately and destroy or burn them

3

Prune bottom 8 inches of foliage to prevent fungal spores splashing from soil

💧 Soil Moisture & Smart Watering Advisor

Quick guide to avoid root asphyxiation and fungus caused by overwatering:

👆 2-Inch Finger Test

Insert your index finger 2 inches into the soil. If it feels cool and damp, DO NOT water. Only water when the top 2 inches feel dry and powdery.

☀️ Morning Watering Rule

Always irrigate early in the morning before 9 AM. Night watering leaves leaves wet in dark conditions, triggering powdery mildew and leaf blight.

🪴 Pot Drainage Holes

Never use decorative pots without bottom drainage holes. Standing water suffocates roots in 48 hours, causing incurable bacterial root rot.
